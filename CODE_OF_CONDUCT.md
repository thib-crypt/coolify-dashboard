# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## What is expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to this, to Coolify, or to
  the language they are writing in.
- Give feedback on the work: what is wrong, why it matters, and what would be better.
- Accept feedback the same way. Disagreement is fine and often useful; contempt is not.
- Respect that maintainers' time is finite and volunteered. A stale pull request is not a
  slight.

## What is not

Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments; discrimination or exclusionary jokes based on
who someone is; sexualised language or attention; publishing someone's private information;
sustained disruption; and deliberately wasting other people's time.

## Scope

This applies in every space belonging to the project — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, code — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/thib-crypt). Reports stay confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may warn, block or
ban a contributor. Decisions are made by the maintainers and explained where doing so does
not expose a reporter.

For a fuller framework, this project's spirit follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
